= Module 4 lab assets: Delivering with AI

Teams of four have one hour to complete the challenge. Learner steps live in `documentation/modules/ROOT/pages/04-aws-lab-provisioning.adoc`.

Create your Ansible project under this directory on your `group_<team_number>` branch.
The lab provides no starter playbooks.
