Accelerating Ansible Delivery with AI

Use Codex or Cursor with Ansible to migrate a Python NetBox installer, compare models and reasoning effort, and deliver a team NetBox project on AWS.

Prerequisites

  • Basic Linux command-line skills and familiarity with YAML and scripting.

  • A RHEL or macOS workstation with a working Ansible installation.

  • Permission to install your chosen AI tool and access to enterprise Codex or an approved Cursor account.

Lab 2’s generation and review steps run on your workstation. Lab 4 requires an AWS environment your team can use for testing and deployment. The workshop does not require OpenShift, AAP, or Showroom services.

Labs

  1. Lab 01: Workspace setup. Configure Codex or Cursor with your local Ansible tools, using the CLI or desktop interface.

  2. Lab 02: Python-to-Ansible NetBox migration. Write your own prompt and make one initial port with Composer in Cursor or Luna in Codex.

  3. Lab 03: Comparing models and effort. Repeat the migration with fixed inputs, change models and effort levels, and compare output, review work, time, and usage.

  4. Lab 04: Customer NetBox delivery project. Migrate the installer and deploy NetBox on AWS as a team. Add hosts in NetBox and query them through Ansible dynamic inventory.

Start the workshop

Begin with Lab 01: Workspace setup. Ask your instructor if you need help.
